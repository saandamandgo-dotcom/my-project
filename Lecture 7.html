Detailed Mitosis, Cell Cycle, Enzymes, and Replication Steps

1. Cell Division Types

أنواع الانقسام الخلوي
Feature Mitosis (الميتوزي) Meiosis (الميوزي)
Occurs in: Somatic Cells (الخلايا الجسدية) Germ Cells (الخلايا التناسلية)
Function: Growth, Regeneration, Repair. Reproduction (Gamete formation).
Outcome: 2 Identical Daughter Cells (2n). 4 Different Daughter Cells (1n).

2. Cell Classification

A. Labile Cells (Proliferative)

Frequently & constantly undergo cell cycle.

خلايا تتجدد باستمرار.

  • Skin
  • Bone marrow (Blood cells)
  • GIT tract cells

B. Stable Cells

Divide only if stimulated (e.g., injury).

خلايا ساكنة، تنقسم عند الحاجة فقط.

  • Liver cells
  • Kidney cells

C. Permanent Cells

Do NOT undergo division.

خلايا لا تنقسم أبداً (تفقد القدرة).

  • Nerve cells
  • Cardiac muscle
  • Skeletal muscle

3. The Cell Cycle Phases

Definition:

Biological process governing cellular replication, growth, and genomic integrity. Includes duplication of parent cell to give two identical daughter cells.

G1
First Gap Phase

Cell growth, duplication of organelles, protein synthesis.

S
Synthesis Phase (The most critical)

DNA Replication occurs here (Genetic material doubles 2n → 4n).

G2
Second Gap Phase

Rapid growth, Spindle formation, preparation for division.

M
Mitotic Phase

Actual Cell Division (Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase).

4. DNA Replication Models

Meselson & Stahl Experiment (Proof)

They used Nitrogen isotopes: Heavy (15N) and Light (14N).

Generation 0 All DNA Heavy (15N)
Generation 1 100% Hybrid (15N-14N)

Disproves Conservative

Generation 2 50% Light (14N) + 50% Hybrid

Disproves Dispersive

Conclusion: Semi-conservative is correct (One old strand + One new strand).

5. Replication Mechanism

الآلية والإنزيمات
Origin of Replication:
  • Prokaryotes: Single origin.
  • Eukaryotes: Multiple origins (to speed up copying large genomes).
1. Unwinding (فك الالتفاف)
  • Helicase: Breaks Hydrogen bonds between bases. Unzips the helix.
  • SSBP: Single Strand Binding Proteins. Keep strands separated.
  • Topoisomerase: Relieves supercoiling tension.
    Has 2 domains: Nuclease (cuts DNA to loosen) & Ligase (reattaches it).
2. Elongation (البناء)
  • Primase: Makes RNA Primers (starting block).
  • DNA Polymerase III:
    • Reads template: 3' to 5'.
    • Synthesizes new DNA: 5' to 3'.
    • Proofreading: Checks for errors (3' to 5' exonuclease activity).
3. Primer Removal & Fusion
  • DNA Polymerase I:
    • Removes RNA primers (Exonuclease).
    • Replaces them with DNA.
    • Proofreads the new segment.
  • Ligase: The "Glue". Joins Okazaki fragments (Phosphodiester bonds).
4. Termination & Telomeres
  • Replication ends when Helicases meet.
  • Telomerase Problem: Lagging strand end cannot be copied normally (chromosomes shorten).
  • Telomerase Enzyme:
    Extends telomeres using its own RNA template (Reverse Transcription: RNA → DNA).
