✂️ Restriction Enzymes in Action

Cut DNA at specific recognition sequences, producing different end types:

🧬 Sticky Ends

Single-stranded overhangs that easily pair with complementary sequences. Example: EcoRI

5'-AATT C-3'
3'-G TTCA-5'

⚡ Blunt Ends

Double-stranded cuts with no overhangs. Example: SmaI

5'-CCC GGG-3'
3'-GGG CCC-5'
الأطراف اللزجة تسهل ربط DNA لأنها تتوافق تكاملياً
EcoRI cutting pattern producing sticky ends
03

Vector Systems

Gene delivery vehicles

🚀 Types of Vectors

Carriers that move genes into host cells:

🧬 Plasmids 🦠 Bacteriophages 📊 BACs 🍞 YACs 🐭 MACs
  • Plasmids: Small circular DNA in bacteria
  • Bacteriophages: Viruses that infect bacteria
  • BACs: Bacterial Artificial Chromosomes
  • YACs: Yeast Artificial Chromosomes
  • MACs: Mammalian Artificial Chromosomes
Plasmid vector structure with origin, marker genes, and cloning sites
04

Gene Cloning Protocol

5-step process from isolation to expression

1

Prepare Gene & Vector

Isolate target gene using restriction enzymes or reverse transcriptase (for cDNA from mRNA).

RESTRICTION_ENZYME: EcoRI
CUT_VECTOR: yes | CUT_INSERT: yes
عزل الجين المطلوب باستخدام الإنزيمات القاطعة
Isolating gene of interest and preparing vector
2

Ligation

Join gene to vector using DNA ligase. Forms phosphodiester bonds to create recombinant DNA.

الربط — إنزيم DNA ligase يربط الجين بالناقل
DNA ligase joining sticky ends
3

Transformation

Introduce recombinant DNA into host cells:

🔥 Chemical

  • CaCl₂ treatment
  • Heat shock: 42°C
  • 30-90 seconds

⚡ Electroporation

  • Electric pulse
  • 15 kV/cm
  • 5 microseconds
Bacterial transformation process
4

Selection & Screening

Use antibiotic resistance markers to identify transformed cells.

Transformed: SURVIVE
Non-transformed: DIE
الانتقاء — الخلايا المحولة فقط تنجو من المضاد الحيوي
Antibiotic resistance selection
5

Purification

Harvest and purify the multiplied rDNA or expressed protein for research, therapeutic, or industrial use.

OUTPUT: recombinant_protein
PURITY: >95% | YIELD: HIGH
05

Applications

Real-world uses of gene cloning

🎯 Main Objectives

  • Identify genes and determine their protein products
  • Store genes in DNA libraries for preservation
  • Produce large amounts of specific proteins (insulin, hormones, vaccines)
  • Create transgenic organisms for agriculture
  • Gene therapy for genetic diseases
