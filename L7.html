Exploring what microbes need to grow (Macronutrients, Micronutrients) and how we count them in the lab.

1. Nutrients

Substances used in biosynthesis and energy release. Required by all microbes.

Macronutrients

Required in large amounts.

C, O, H, N, S, P, K, Ca, Mg
  • Carbon: 50% of dry weight.
  • N, S, P: For DNA, RNA, Proteins.

مغذيات كبرى: يحتاجها الميكروب بكميات كبيرة لبناء الخلايا.

Micronutrients

Trace elements. Required in minute amounts.

Mn, Zn, Co, Mo, Ni, Fe, Cu

Function: Enzyme Cofactors (Aid in catalysis).

مغذيات صغرى (عناصر شحيحة): تعمل كمساعدات للإنزيمات.

Growth Factors

Organic compounds needed in small amounts.

  • Vitamins: Function as Coenzymes.
  • Amino acids, Purines, Pyrimidines.

عوامل النمو: مركبات عضوية مثل الفيتامينات.

2. Nutritional Classification

Criteria Type Source
Energy Source Phototrophs Light (Sunlight).
Chemotrophs Chemicals (Organic or Inorganic).
Carbon Source Autotrophs $CO_2$ (Self-feeders).
Heterotrophs Organic compounds (Feed on others).
Example: Humans are Chemoheterotrophs. Plants are Photoautotrophs.

3. Culture Media

Nutrient material prepared for microbial growth.

By Physical State

  • Liquid (Broth): No solidifying agent. For growing large numbers.
  • Solid (Agar): Contains Agar (seaweed extract). Used to isolate colonies.

By Chemical Composition

  • Chemically Defined (Synthetic): Exact formula is known.
  • Complex: Unknown exact composition (contains Yeast extract, Peptone).
Selective Media

Suppress unwanted microbes and encourage desired ones.

Differential Media

Make it easier to distinguish colonies of different microbes.

Enrichment Media

Increases numbers of desired microbes to detectable levels.

4. Measuring Microbial Growth

Direct Methods

1. Plate Count (CFU)

Counts Viable (living) cells only. Includes Pour Plate & Spread Plate.

2. Filtration

For samples with low bacteria count (e.g., water quality).

3. Direct Microscopic Count

Uses Petroff-Hausser chamber. Counts Dead + Live cells. Fast but less accurate.

Indirect Methods

1. Turbidity

Cloudiness measured by Spectrophotometer (Absorbance). More cells = More light blocked.

قياس التعكر باستخدام مقياس الطيف الضوئي.

2. Metabolic Activity

Measuring production of $CO_2$ or acid.

3. Dry Weight

Best for filamentous organisms like Fungi.

Most Probable Number (MPN): Statistical estimating technique. Useful when microbes won't grow on solid media.
