What is Bioinformatics?

Bioinformatics is an interdisciplinary field that develops methods and software tools for understanding biological data. It combines:

Biology
Computer Science
Information Engineering
Mathematics
Statistics

"Uses computer science tools to answer biological questions by processing tremendous amounts of data"

Primary Sequence Databases (INSDC)

N

GenBank

NCBI - USA

NIH genetic sequence database with annotated DNA sequences

• 259M+ sequences (Dec 2025)

• 665B+ base pairs

E

EMBL-ENA

Europe

European Nucleotide Archive - comprehensive DNA/RNA sequence repository

• Part of EBI

• Daily data exchange

D

DDBJ

Japan

DNA Data Bank of Japan - National Institute of Genetics

• NIG Collaboration

• Asia-Pacific hub

All three databases exchange data daily - containing the same underlying data with different organization and annotation

Protein Databases

UniProtKB

Universal Protein Resource - The central hub for protein sequence and annotation data

Swiss-Prot

Reviewed, manually annotated

TrEMBL

Unreviewed, automatically annotated

Sequence Analysis

Fundamental algorithms for comparing DNA, RNA, and protein sequences to identify functional and evolutionary relationships.

Substitution

One amino acid/nucleotide replaced by another

Deletion

Loss of sequence segment

Insertion

Addition of new sequence segment

Conservation

Identical sequences across species (functional importance)
