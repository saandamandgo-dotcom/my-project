Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)

The revolutionary technique made possible by Taq DNA Polymerase, a thermostable enzyme from Thermus aquaticus that survives repeated heating to 95°C.

1

Denaturation

95-98°C - DNA strands separate

2

Annealing

50-60°C - Primers bind to template

3

Extension

72°C - Taq polymerase synthesizes new strand

20-40 Cycles

Taq DNA Polymerase

EC 2.7.7.7 | Thermus aquaticus
  • 5'→3' Polymerase activity: Synthesizes DNA
  • 5'→3' Exonuclease: Removes RNA primers
  • No 3'→5' proofreading: Error-prone
  • Terminal transferase: Adds single A overhang

Best for: PCR amplification, TA cloning

T4 DNA Polymerase

EC 2.7.7.7 | Bacteriophage T4
  • 5'→3' Polymerase: DNA synthesis
  • 3'→5' Exonuclease: Strong proofreading
  • No 5'→3' exonuclease: No strand displacement
  • High fidelity: Accurate synthesis

Best for: Blunt-end generation, gap filling, probe labeling

Poly(A) Polymerase vs TA Cloning

Poly(A) Polymerase

  • • Works on RNA
  • • Adds many A's (50-250)
  • • Uses ATP
  • • Regulates mRNA stability
  • • Cellular function
VS

TA Cloning

  • • Works on DNA
  • • Adds single A
  • • Uses Taq polymerase
  • • Cloning technique
  • • Laboratory method

Key Point: Both are adenylation reactions at 3'-OH, but differ in substrate (RNA vs DNA) and purpose (regulation vs cloning).

TA Cloning: A-overhang on insert pairs with T-overhang on vector

Kinases & Phosphatases

The Molecular On/Off Switches

مفاتيح التشغيل والإيقاف الجزيئية

🔴

Kinases

The "ON" Switch

Add phosphate groups (PO₄)

Using ATP as phosphate donor

Activate proteins

Change protein conformation/function

Target amino acids

Serine, Threonine, Tyrosine, Histidine

EC Class: 2.7.x.x (Transferases)

🔵

Phosphatases

The "OFF" Switch

Remove phosphate groups

Using water (hydrolysis)

Deactivate proteins

Return to original state

Hydrolysis reaction

Water breaks phosphate ester bond

EC Class: 3.x.x.x (Hydrolases)

Phosphorylation cascade: Kinases add phosphates to activate signaling pathways; Phosphatases remove them to terminate signals

Key Takeaways

DNA polymerases synthesize DNA in 5'→3' direction using dNTPs

Taq polymerase is thermostable (95°C) for PCR

T4 polymerase has 3'→5' proofreading (high fidelity)

Reverse transcriptase makes DNA from RNA

Kinases add phosphates (ON); Phosphatases remove (OFF)

Poly(A) polymerase works on RNA; TA cloning on DNA

These enzymes are the foundation of modern genetic engineering, enabling precise DNA manipulation for research, medicine, and biotechnology.
