Understanding Binary Fission, Generation Time, The Growth Curve, and Environmental Factors (Temp, pH, O2).

1. Growth & Division

Definition

In microbiology, Growth refers to the increase in the number of cells (population), not the size of a single cell.

النمو في الميكروبيولوجي يعني زيادة عدد الخلايا (التكاثر) وليس حجم الخلية الواحدة.

Binary Fission

Bacteria reproduce by Binary Fission (One cell divides into two identical cells).

  1. DNA Replication: Chromosome copies itself.
  2. Elongation: Cell grows, chromosomes separate.
  3. Septum Formation: Cross wall forms.
  4. Separation: Two daughter cells separate.

Generation Time (Doubling Time)

Time required for a population to double in number.

Nt = N0 × 2ⁿ

Example: E. coli generation time is ~20 minutes.

2. The Bacterial Growth Curve

Growth of bacteria in a closed system (Batch Culture) follows 4 phases.

1. Lag

Adaptation Phase

No increase in cell number. Cells are active, synthesizing enzymes and DNA.

طور التأقلم: لا زيادة في العدد، فقط نشاط داخلي.

2. Log

Exponential Phase

Maximum growth rate. Population doubles at constant rate. Most sensitive to antibiotics.

الطور اللوغاريتمي: أقصى سرعة للنمو والتضاعف.

3. Stationary

Equilibrium Phase

Growth rate = Death rate. Nutrients deplete, toxins accumulate.

طور الثبات: معدل النمو يساوي معدل الموت.

4. Death

Decline Phase

Cells die exponentially. Death rate > Growth rate.

طور الموت: تتناقص الخلايا بسرعة.

3. Temperature

Bacteria have Minimum, Optimum, and Maximum growth temperatures.

Psychrophiles

Cold Lovers

-5°C to 15°C

Grow in refrigerator, cause food spoilage.

Mesophiles

Moderate Lovers

20°C to 45°C

Most human pathogens (Body temp 37°C).

Danger Zone: 15-50°C

Thermophiles

Heat Lovers

45°C to 80°C

Hot springs, compost.

4. pH & Oxygen

pH (Acidity)

  • Acidophiles: Grow at low pH (Acidic). pH < 5.5
  • Neutrophiles: Most bacteria & pathogens. pH 6.5 - 7.5
  • Alkaliphiles: Grow at high pH (Basic). pH > 8.5

Oxygen Requirement

Obligate Aerobes: Require O2 (Grow at top of tube).
Obligate Anaerobes: Killed by O2 (Grow at bottom). Ex: Clostridium.
Facultative Anaerobes: Grow better with O2, but can grow without it. Ex: E. coli.
Microaerophiles: Need low concentration of O2.

5. Osmotic Pressure

Effect of salt and sugar concentrations (Water activity).

Isotonic
Normal Cell

Solute inside = Solute outside. No change.

Hypertonic (High Salt)
Shriveled Cell

Water leaves cell -> Plasmolysis (Cytoplasm shrinks). Used in food preservation (Salting).

يسبب انكماش السيتوبلازم (Plasmolysis).

Halophiles
Happy in Salt

Bacteria that require high salt (e.g., in Dead Sea).
Facultative Halophiles: Tolerate salt (Staph. aureus).
