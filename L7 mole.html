🎯 Translation: Three Distinct Phases | الترجمة: ثلاث مراحل مميزة

Translation in all cells is characterized by three distinct phases that work in sequence: Initiation, where the translation machinery assembles; Elongation, where amino acids are added; and Termination, where the polypeptide chain is released.
الترجمة في جميع الخلايا تتميز بثلاث مراحل مميزة تعمل تسلسلياً: البدء، حيث تتجمع آلية الترجمة؛ الإطالة، حيث تُضاف الأحماض الأمينية؛ والإنهاء، حيث يُطلق سلسلة البوليببتيد.

🔑 Key Components Required for Translation Initiation | المكونات الرئيسية لبدء الترجمة

🎯 1. mRNA

Contains AUG start codon
Has Shine-Dalgarno sequence (prokaryotes)
Kozak sequence (eukaryotes)
Carries genetic code for protein

⚙️ 2. Ribosomal Subunits

Prokaryotes: 30S + 50S → 70S ribosome
Eukaryotes: 40S + 60S → 80S ribosome
Provide structural framework

🎯 3. Initiator tRNA

Specific charged tRNA (fMet-tRNAfMet)
Reads AUG start codon
Carries formylated methionine
Different from elongation tRNAs

🔧 4. Initiation Factors

IF-1, IF-2, IF-3 (prokaryotes)
eIF1, eIF2, eIF3... (eukaryotes)
G-protein family members
Use GTP hydrolysis energy

⚡ 5. GTP

Energy for initiation
Hydrolyzed to GDP + Pi
Essential for complex assembly
Multiple GTP molecules used

🎯 Critical Note | ملاحظة حاسمة:

  • Initiator tRNA (tRNAfMet) reads ONLY the start codon (AUG) and NOT internal AUG codons
  • In eukaryotes, tRNA carries non-formylated methionine (tRNAMet)
  • In prokaryotes, tRNA carries formyl-methionine (fMet-tRNAfMet)

🎯 Svedberg Units | وحدات سفيدبيرغ:

  • Measure sedimentation coefficient (rate in centrifuge)
  • NOT strictly additive (depends on shape AND mass)
  • 30S + 50S = 70S (not 80S)
  • 40S + 60S = 80S (not 100S)

🎯 A-P-E Sites Function | وظيفة المواقع A-P-E:

  • ✅ Order of tRNA movement: A → P → E
  • ✅ A Site: Decoding (codon-anticodon recognition)
  • ✅ P Site: Peptide bond formation (catalytic center)
  • ✅ E Site: Prevents premature re-entry of empty tRNA

🎯 Initiator tRNA (tRNAfMet) | tRNA المبدئ

Initiator tRNA is specialized to read only the start codon (AUG) and not internal AUG codons. It carries N-formylmethionine in prokaryotes and methionine in eukaryotes.
tRNA المبدئ متخصص لقراءة كودون البدء فقط (AUG) وليس الكودونات الداخلية AUG. يحمل الميثيونين المفورميل في بدائيات النواة والميثيونين في حقيقيات النواة.

🎯 Key Feature | الميزة الرئيسية:

  • Specificity: Initiator tRNA recognizes ONLY start codon in proper context
  • Not used for elongation: Cannot participate in peptide chain elongation
  • Formylation: Blocks amino group, prevents peptide bond formation in wrong direction

🔧 Soluble Protein Factors & GTP | عوامل البروتين الذائبة و GTP

Initiation factors are G-protein family members that use energy from GTP hydrolysis. IF-2-GTP in prokaryotes and eIF2-GTP in eukaryotes provide energy for initiation complex assembly.
عوامل البدء هي أفراد من عائلة البروتين G التي تستخدم طاقة من تحلل GTP. IF-2-GTP في بدائيات النواة و eIF2-GTP في حقيقيات النواة توفر الطاقة لتجميع معقد البدء.
Factor | العامل Prokaryotes | بدائيات النواة Eukaryotes | حقيقيات النواة Function | الوظيفة
Initiation GTPase IF-2-GTP eIF2-GTP Binds initiator tRNA, provides energy for assembly
Subunit Factors IF-1, IF-3 eIF1, eIF1A, eIF3, others Facilitate small subunit binding to mRNA and tRNA
Energy Source GTP → GDP + Pi (hydrolysis) Essential for all IF/eIF functions

⚡ Energy Requirement | متطلبات الطاقة:

  • Multiple GTP molecules: Required per initiation event
  • Hydrolysis timing: Carefully controlled by initiation factors
  • Recycling: GDP-GTP exchange factors regenerate active GTP-bound form

🧬 Formation of Initiation Complex | تكوين معقد البدء

The initiation complex forms in several stages: preinitiation complex assembly, initiator tRNA binding, and finally addition of the large ribosomal subunit to form the complete ribosome.
معقد البدء يتكون في عدة مراحل: تجميع المعقد قبل البدء، ارتباط tRNA المبدئ، وأخيراً إضافة الوحدة الريبوسومية الكبيرة لتكوين الريبوسوم الكامل.

🎯 Final Complex Composition | تركيبة المعقد النهائية:

  • Complete ribosome: 70S (30S + 50S) in prokaryotes
  • mRNA: Positioned with AUG codon in P site
  • Initiator tRNA: fMet-tRNAfMet in P site (positioned correctly)
  • A site: Empty and ready for first aminoacyl-tRNA
  • E site: Empty

⚖️ Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic Translation | مقارنة بين بدائيات وحقيقيات النواة

Feature | الميزة Prokaryotes | بدائيات النواة Eukaryotes | حقيقيات النواة
Ribosome Size 70S (30S + 50S) 80S (40S + 60S)
Initiator tRNA fMet-tRNAfMet (formylated) Met-tRNA (non-formylated)
Initiation Signal Shine-Dalgarno sequence Kozak sequence (CCRCCAUGG)
mRNA Recognition Direct binding to SD sequence Scanning from 5' cap to first AUG
Initiation Factors 3 factors (IF-1, IF-2, IF-3) Many factors (eIF1, eIF2, eIF3, eIF4A, etc.)
Termination Factors 3 release factors (RF1, RF2, RF3) Single factor pair (eRF1 + eRF3)

⚠️ Clinical Significance | الأهمية السريرية:

Antibiotic targets: Many antibiotics exploit differences between prokaryotic and eukaryotic translation machinery

  • Tetracycline: Binds 30S subunit, blocks tRNA entry
  • Streptomycin: Causes misreading of codons
  • Chloramphenicol: Inhibits peptidyl transferase (50S)
  • Erythromycin: Blocks elongation on 50S subunit

🎯 Why This Matters | لماذا هذا مهم:

Selective toxicity: Bacterial 70S ribosomes are targeted by antibiotics without harming eukaryotic 80S ribosomes (in human cells)

Design principle: Key differences allow development of antibacterial drugs

🧠 Memory Aids & Exam Tips | وسائل الحفظ ونصائح الامتحان

🤔 Mnemonics for Translation Initiation | حفظ بدء الترجمة:

  • "30S + 50S = 70S, 40S + 60S = 80S" - Remember ribosome sizes
  • "SD in Pro, Kozak in Euk" - Initiation signals
  • "fMet in bacteria, Met in human" - Initiator tRNA
  • "A-P-E = Accept, Park, Exit" - Ribosome sites
  • "3 IFs vs many eIFs" - Factor complexity

⚠️ Common Exam Questions | أسئلة الامتحان الشائعة:

  • Why can't DNA polymerase initiate without primer but RNA polymerase can? Different enzyme mechanisms
  • Why is Shine-Dalgarno not found in eukaryotes? Different ribosomal RNA sequences
  • What happens if initiation factors are missing? Translation cannot start
  • How many GTPs used in initiation? At least 1-2 per initiation event

🎯 Clinical Correlations | الارتباطات السريرية:

Genetic diseases: Mutations in initiation factors cause severe developmental disorders

Cancer: Overexpression of initiation factors promotes tumor growth

Viral infection: Some viruses hijack host initiation machinery

Antibiotics: Target bacterial translation initiation selectively
